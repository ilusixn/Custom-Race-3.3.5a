# Racials by race

Taken from the installed client (`CharacterCreate.lua`). Races marked **(new, subject to change)** are the AllRaces v12 additions; they currently borrow the racials of the race named in brackets (Elune's Chosen and Qiraji have their own), so everything on them can still change.

## Alpha Goblin

- **Rocket Jump** (active): Every goblin carries a rocket for emergencies, a Tinker's favorite. Escapes any immobilization or movement slowing effect.
- **Rocket Barrage** (active): When in doubt, fire everything. Increases attack and casting speed for a short time.
- **Time is Money** (passive): A goblin never wastes a swing. Increases chance to hit by 1%.
- **Best Deals Anywhere** (passive): Every faction is a customer. Reputation gains increased by 10%.

## Alpha Worgen

- **Thick Pelt** (passive): The wild hardened them. Reduces Nature damage taken by 4%.
